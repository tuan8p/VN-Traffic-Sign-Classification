# Contributing

1. Use your role branch (`feat/svm`, `feat/boosting`, `feat/dl`, `feat/preprocessing`, `feat/demo`).
2. Fill `TODO(team):` markers. Do not change `configs/shared.yaml` locks without group vote.
3. Keep offline preprocess artifacts identical across pipelines.
4. Training must use W&B; preprocess/EDA/demo must not require it.
5. PR into `main` with reproduce steps.
